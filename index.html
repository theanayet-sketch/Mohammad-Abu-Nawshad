Who I Am

About Me

I am Mohammad Abu Nawshad, a seasoned Senior Merchandiser with over 8 years of experience in Bangladesh's garment export industry. I currently lead the merchandising team at GMS+ (Global Management Services Private Ltd.) in Uttara, Dhaka, where I manage end-to-end buyer relationships for premium European fashion brands.

My career spans woven garment merchandising for both men's and women's departments, with deep expertise in ladies' light woven fabrics and shirts. I have successfully managed accounts with renowned international buyers including Peek & Cloppenburg, BESTSELLER, US POLO, LEE, JACK WILLIS, ADLER, JEANS FRITZ, ESOTIQ, and The Edinburgh Woollen Mill.

I hold a Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) in Accounting from ASA University Bangladesh, which gives me a strong foundation in cost analysis, negotiation, and financial planning within the supply chain context. I am currently based in Kongsberg, Norway, as an international student pursuing a Master's degree in Sustainable Management at the University of South Eastern Norway.
